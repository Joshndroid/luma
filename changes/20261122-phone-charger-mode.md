Summary: Phones charge when plugged in while off
Details: A phone that turns on because a charger was plugged in now charges with the screen off instead of starting. Press the power key to see the battery level, or hold it for two seconds to start the phone. The phone turns off when the charger is removed. When the battery is empty on a charger too weak for the phone, it now shuts down cleanly instead of losing power.
