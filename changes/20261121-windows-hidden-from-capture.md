Summary: Apps can keep a window out of screen sharing
Details: A recorder or streaming app can keep its own window out of screen shares and screenshots, so sharing the screen it is on no longer fills the share with copies of itself. The window stays on your screen, you can still share it on its own, and the share picker marks it "Hidden from screen sharing".
