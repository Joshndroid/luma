Summary: One set of permissions for every app
Details: Apps installed from a file, from Depot or from Flathub now get file access that fits what they are: a music player gets Music, a photo editor Pictures, an office suite Documents, a file manager all your files. Other folders refuse access instead of looking empty, a refused folder can be allowed once without restarting the app, and keys, passwords, browser profiles and other apps' data stay out of reach.
